## Brief

but struggle with grammar and argument development. Job: begin useful self-revision
without reading a long manual. Preserve ASHYQ red/ink/offwhite, real logo/header,
normal-width approved sans and rounded corners. No AI score/personal improvement
claim. Public ready essays first; own-text gated workshop separate and honest.

## Divergent territories considered
A. Revision proof (material of writing): the page is one annotated proof; a word
moves from the student's revision into an adjacent explanation. Anchor: ASHYQ's
attempt-before-explanation teaching loop. O/F/code/portability:4/5/5/3.
B. Open path (identity/process): one red route passes through four rubric stages.
Anchor: correcting one aspect at a time.4/4/5/4; too close to catalogue/history.
C. Two drafts (formal system): one sentence occupies exactly the same coordinates
in before/after views. Anchor: comparison is central to the trainer.3/5/5/4;
strong comparison but less flexible for whole-essay entry.
Choose A, borrow only C's spatial continuity. No new font, badges or generic grid.

## Concept card
Idea: Сильное эссе. По одной правке.
Metaphor: a writer's revision proof with one useful margin explanation.
Signature: selected word stays in its line; the revision becomes the anchor for
its explanation, then returns to the complete sentence. Native user action
controls actual exercise feedback; passive landing illustration explicitly
labelled as illustrative and not taken from either teaching essay.
Emotion: I understand the action and can start now.
Motion verb: revise in place.
Screenshot: one large English sentence with a precise red edit and nearby rule.
