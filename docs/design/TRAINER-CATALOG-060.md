## Selected concept

Idea: Pick a difficulty in familiar words; see one relevant exercise and what it teaches.
Anchor: ASHYQ already has a 10-need selector and eleven actual trainer destinations. The redesign makes that existing mapping tangible.
Signature: selected difficulty maintains spatial continuity as the recommended exercise expands below; key evidence underlines once, then rests. No forced auto-navigation or automatic completion animation.
Emotion: clarity and readiness to attempt.
Motion verb: unfold.
Screenshot: selected FALSE/NOT GIVEN difficulty plus a contrasting Reading exercise demonstration.

Palette: existing warm off-white, scarlet, black; muted neutral metadata. Normal-width sans serif; large but not condensed headings. Existing 28px radius and pill actions.
Phone: page-flow controls, one column, no pinned or sideways forced scroll. Full catalogue and saved-work access remain in the page. All ten needs must remain discoverable, even though the static composition previews only three.
Desktop adaptation: difficulty choices and featured recommendation side by side; full catalogue below. Not depicted by this mobile-only proposal.

Content: all shown trainer destinations exist. Sample English sentence is an explicitly labelled illustrative micro-example, not a diagnostic result or scored student answer. Never pretend guest has a known weakness. Personalization from diagnostic results belongs to separately owned task057; this concept must work without it.

Review boundaries: static art can establish layout and copy direction, not motion quality, performance, real tap behavior, focus/accessibility, or successful deployment. No Awwwards certification or numerical implementation score from a bitmap.

Next: user selects direction, then publish an implementation claim and preserve current routes, all11 directions, the10-need mapping, filters, history and no-provider promises.
